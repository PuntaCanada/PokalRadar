# Remaining historical home-right evidence

The 12 additional observations are recorded in `data/home-right-only.json`, not as complete mathematical draw fields. The probabilities remain null. Sources are recorded per group in that file.

- 2020/21 round 3: the draw included unresolved participants from postponed second-round matches. The eventual pairings are recorded separately from the original draw field; no unconditional probability is inferred.
- 2020/21 round 5: Werder Bremen / RB Leipzig was drawn before the outstanding quarter-final was resolved. The original slot and eventual club are not interchangeable for probability calculations.
- 2021/22 round 1: BFC Dynamo represents the subsequently confirmed Berlin cup entrant. Do not retrospectively treat the confirmed club as a known draw participant.
- 2023/24 round 5: the Saarbrücken / Mönchengladbach quarter-final was outstanding at the draw; the home right against Kaiserslautern depended on the winner. Record the realized Saarbrücken home right, not a hypothetical draw probability. Leverkusen / Düsseldorf is also retained as home-right-only evidence rather than attempting a partial-field probability model.

League-tier evidence is season-specific, including clubs that withdrew during a season. The tier describes the competition entered, not the final league position.
